## Current grammar focus

At this stage, I am focusing on Past Simple.

I already practiced positive sentences:

- I went to work.
- I studied English.
- I drank coffee.
- I woke up early.
- I bought a new Wi-Fi adapter.

I also practiced negative sentences:

- I didn’t go.
- I didn’t study.
- I didn’t drink coffee.
- I didn’t work on my project.
- The adapter didn’t connect to my router.

I also practiced questions:

- Did you wake up early today?
- Did you drink coffee today?
- Did you study English yesterday?
- Did you take a shower after getting out of bed?

Important rule:

After did / didn’t, use the base verb.

Correct:

- I didn’t go.
- Did you go?

Wrong:

- I didn’t went.
- Did you went?

## Recent important corrections

Wrong:

- I done everything yesterday.

Correct:

- I did everything yesterday.

Wrong:

- I have done everything yesterday.

Correct:

- I did everything yesterday.

Wrong:

## Important learning note

I know Polish very well, and sometimes Polish grammar influences my English.

Examples:

Wrong:

- I listen music.

Correct:

- I listen to music.

Wrong:

- I work on administration.

Correct:

- I work in administration.

Wrong:

- We looked for my Honda.

Correct:

- We looked at my Honda.

Because:

- look for = search
- look at = inspect / watch
