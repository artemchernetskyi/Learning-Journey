## Important Polish / Ukrainian interference

Artem knows Polish very well, and Polish grammar sometimes affects his English.

Examples to repeat and correct:

Wrong:

- I listen music.

Correct:

- I listen to music.

Wrong:

- I work on administration.

Correct:

- I work in administration.

Wrong:

- We looked for my Honda.

Correct:

- We looked at my Honda.

Explanation:

- look for = search
- look at = inspect / watch

## Current grammar focus

Current focus:

Past Simple — negatives and questions.

Artem already practiced:

Positive:

- I went to work.
- I studied English.
- I drank coffee.
- I woke up early.
- I bought a new Wi-Fi adapter.

Negative:

- I didn’t go.
- I didn’t study.
- I didn’t drink coffee.
- I didn’t work on my project.
- The adapter didn’t connect to my router.

Questions:

- Did you wake up early today?
- Did you drink coffee today?
- Did you study English yesterday?
- Did you take a shower after getting out of bed?

Important rule:

After did / didn’t, use the base verb.

Correct:

- I didn’t go.
- Did you go?

Wrong:

- I didn’t went.
- Did you went?
