# English Mistakes — Artem

## Purpose

This file contains my personal English mistakes and corrections.

The goal is not to be perfect immediately. The goal is to notice repeated mistakes, understand them, and slowly remove them from my English.

---

## Main rule

Do not correct everything at once.

Focus on the most important repeated mistakes.

---

## Past Simple mistakes

### Mistake 1

Wrong:

- I done everything yesterday.

Correct:

- I did everything yesterday.

Explanation:

Use Past Simple with a specific time in the past, for example:

- yesterday
- last week
- two days ago

---

### Mistake 2

Wrong:

- I have done everything yesterday.

Correct:

- I did everything yesterday.

Explanation:

Do not use Present Perfect with a specific past time like "yesterday".

Correct:

- I did everything yesterday.
- I have done everything.

---

### Mistake 3

Wrong:

- I didn’t went.
- Did you went?

Correct:

- I didn’t go.
- Did you go?

Explanation:

After did / didn’t, use the base verb.

---

## Preposition mistakes

### Mistake 4

Wrong:

- The adapter didn’t connect with my router.

Correct:

- The adapter didn’t connect to my router.

---

### Mistake 5

Wrong:

- I tried to fix it four hours.

Correct:

- I tried to fix it for four hours.

Explanation:

Use "for" when you say how long something lasted.

Examples:

- for four hours
- for 30 minutes
- for two days

---

## Word choice mistakes

### Mistake 6

Wrong:

- I adapt my Wi-Fi adapter.

Correct:

- I set up my Wi-Fi adapter.

Explanation:

set up = налаштувати

---

### Mistake 7

Wrong:

- After than I went home.

Correct:

- After that, I went home.

---

### Mistake 8

Wrong:

- My team started they work.

Correct:

- My team started their work.

Explanation:

they = вони  
their = їхній / їхня / їхнє

---

## Natural English phrases

Use these phrases more often:

- I set up my Wi-Fi adapter.
- I tried to fix the problem.
- It didn’t work.
- The adapter didn’t connect to my router.
- I worked on my project.
- I did everything I had planned.
- After that, I went home.
- I was tired because I had tried to fix it for four hours.
