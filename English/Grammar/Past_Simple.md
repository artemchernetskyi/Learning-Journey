# Past Simple — Notes

## Purpose

This file contains my notes about Past Simple.

Past Simple is important because I often need to talk about:

- yesterday
- my workday
- problems I solved
- what I did on Ubuntu
- what I learned
- technical troubleshooting

---

## When to use Past Simple

Use Past Simple for finished actions in the past.

Common signal words:

- yesterday
- last night
- last week
- last weekend
- two days ago
- in 2020
- then
- after that
- finally

Examples:

- Yesterday I went to work.
- Last night I studied English.
- Two days ago I installed Git.
- After that, I opened VS Code.
- Finally, I made my first Git commit.

---

## Positive sentences

Formula:

Subject + past verb

Examples:

- I went to work.
- I drank coffee.
- I studied English.
- I installed Git.
- I created a project folder.
- I opened VS Code.
- I made my first commit.

---

## Negative sentences

Formula:

Subject + didn’t + base verb

Examples:

- I didn’t go to work.
- I didn’t drink coffee.
- I didn’t study English.
- I didn’t work on my project.
- The adapter didn’t connect to my router.

Important rule:

After didn’t, use the base verb.

Correct:

- I didn’t go.
- I didn’t drink.
- I didn’t study.

Wrong:

- I didn’t went.
- I didn’t drank.
- I didn’t studied.

---

## Questions

Formula:

Did + subject + base verb?

Examples:

- Did you go to work?
- Did you drink coffee?
- Did you study English?
- Did you install Git?
- Did you open VS Code?
- Did the adapter connect to your router?

Important rule:

After did, use the base verb.

Correct:

- Did you go?
- Did you drink?
- Did you study?

Wrong:

- Did you went?
- Did you drank?
- Did you studied?

---

## Short answers

Examples:

Question:

Did you study English yesterday?

Short answers:

- Yes, I did.
- No, I didn’t.

Full answers:

- Yes, I studied English yesterday.
- No, I didn’t study English yesterday.

## Common mistakes

### Mistake 1

Wrong:

- I done everything yesterday.

Correct:

- I did everything yesterday.

---

### Mistake 2

Wrong:

- I have done everything yesterday.

Correct:

- I did everything yesterday.

Explanation:

Use Past Simple with a specific past time like yesterday.

---

### Mistake 3

Wrong:

- I didn’t went.

Correct:

- I didn’t go.

---

### Mistake 4

Wrong:

- Did you went?

Correct:

- Did you go?
